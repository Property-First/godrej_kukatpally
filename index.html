About us

Godrej Kukatpally

Godrej Kukatpally is a pre-launch high-rise residential project by Godrej Properties located in Kukatpally, Hyderabad. Spread across 7.8 acres, it offers a modern lifestyle with 70% open green spaces. The project features thoughtfully designed 3 and 4 BHK apartments for comfortable living. It enjoys excellent connectivity to NH 65, HITEC City, JNTU, and major roads like Inner Ring Road and Nehru ORR. The location is close to key IT hubs such as Gachibowli, Madhapur, and Financial District. Currently in the pre-launch stage, it is expected to be ready for possession by December 2029, making it a promising investment opportunity.
